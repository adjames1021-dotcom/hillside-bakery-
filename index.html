How to bake

  1. Grab ingredients from the pantry on the left. Click and drag them.
  2. Drop them in the mixing bowl to match a recipe, then press Mix!
  3. Put the dough in the oven. Take it out once it's golden, before it burns!
  4. Serve customers by dropping (or throwing!) what they ask for onto them.
  5. Spend coins in the Shop on new recipes and upgrades.

Tips: scroll or pinch to zoom, drag empty space to look around, press R while holding something to spin it, and toss burnt treats in the bin.
